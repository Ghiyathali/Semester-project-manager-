# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people newer to the tools than you are
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use this software
- Assuming good faith

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when someone is
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a confidential issue or contacting a maintainer directly.
Reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be
respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or
permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they consider inappropriate.
